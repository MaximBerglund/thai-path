# Lesson 2: Names and Introductions

## Grammar
| ID | Title | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| grammar-02-pronouns | First-person pronouns | Use ผม or ฉัน for "I" depending on speaker preference and context. |
| grammar-02-name-pattern | Naming pattern | The pattern ผมชื่อ... / ฉันชื่อ... means "My name is...". |

## Vocabulary
| ID | Thai | English | Transliteration | Part of Speech | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vocab-02-phom | ผม | I | phom | pronoun | Common for male speakers. |
| vocab-02-chan | ฉัน | I | chan | pronoun | Common neutral/female self-reference in lessons. |
| vocab-02-chue | ชื่อ | name; to be named | chue | noun/verb | Used in introductions. |
| vocab-02-yin-dii | ยินดีที่ได้รู้จัก | nice to meet you | yin dii thii dai ruu jak | phrase | Formal and friendly. |

## Example Sentences
| ID | Thai | English | Transliteration | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sentence-02-my-name-dan | ผมชื่อแดนครับ | My name is Dan. | phom chue Dan khrap | Introduction with ผม. |
| sentence-02-my-name-malee | ฉันชื่อมาลีค่ะ | My name is Malee. | chan chue Maa-lii kha | Introduction with ฉัน. |
| sentence-02-nice-to-meet-you | ยินดีที่ได้รู้จักครับ | Nice to meet you. | yin dii thii dai ruu jak khrap | Polite phrase. |
