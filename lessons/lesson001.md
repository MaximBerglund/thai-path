# Lesson 1 – Building Your First Thai Sentences

Welcome to Thai Path!

The goal of this course is **not** to memorise isolated words.

Instead, you'll learn the building blocks of Thai so that you can start creating your own sentences from the very beginning.

---

# Grammar

## Thai Word Order

One of the nicest things about Thai is that the basic sentence structure is almost identical to English.

Subject → Verb → Object

Example:

English:

> I eat rice.

Thai:

> ฉันกินข้าว

Romanization:

> chan gin kâao

Word by word:

| Thai | Romanization | English |
|------|--------------|----------|
| ฉัน | chan | I |
| กิน | gin | eat |
| ข้าว | kâao | rice / food |

This is the pattern we'll use throughout the course.

---

## Grammar Rule #1

### Thai verbs never change.

Compare English:

- I eat
- He eats
- We ate
- They are eating

In Thai:

กิน

(gin)

always stays exactly the same.

Thai uses separate time words instead of changing the verb.

This makes Thai grammar considerably simpler than English.

# Vocabulary

## Pronouns

| Thai | Romanization | English | Notes |
|------|--------------|----------|------|
| ฉัน | chan | I | Neutral and commonly used in beginner material. |
| คุณ | khun | you | Polite and extremely useful. |
| เขา | khao | he / she | Same word for both genders. |
| เรา | rao | we | Common everyday word. |
| พวกเขา | phûak-khăo | they | Literally "their group". |

# Example Sentences

| Thai | Romanization | English |
|------|--------------|----------|
| ฉันกินข้าว | chan gin kâao | I eat rice. |
| ฉันดื่มน้ำ | chan dèum náam | I drink water. |
| คุณพูดภาษาไทย | khun phûut phaa-săa Thai | You speak Thai. |
| เขาไปบ้าน | khao bpai bâan | He goes home. / She goes home. |
| เรามากินข้าว | rao maa gin kâao | We come to eat. |

---

# Grammar Notes

## No articles

Thai has no words corresponding to **a**, **an**, or **the**.

English:

> I drink the water.

Thai:

> ฉันดื่มน้ำ

Literally:

> I drink water.

---

## No verb conjugation

Remember:

กิน

always means "eat".

The verb itself never changes.
