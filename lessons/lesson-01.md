# Lesson 1: Greetings and Polite Particles

## Grammar
| ID | Title | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| grammar-01-politeness-particles | Polite particles | Thai statements often end with polite particles: ครับ for many male speakers and ค่ะ for many female speakers. |
| grammar-01-sawatdii | สวัสดี | The word สวัสดี works for hello and goodbye. |

## Vocabulary
| ID | Thai | English | Transliteration | Part of Speech | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| vocab-01-sawatdii | สวัสดี | hello | sawat dii | greeting | Use at any time of day. |
| vocab-01-khop-khun | ขอบคุณ | thank you | khop khun | phrase | Add a polite particle in formal speech. |
| vocab-01-khrap | ครับ | polite particle | khrap | particle | Often used by male speakers. |
| vocab-01-kha | ค่ะ | polite particle | kha | particle | Often used by female speakers. |

## Example Sentences
| ID | Thai | English | Transliteration | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sentence-01-hello-khrap | สวัสดีครับ | Hello. | sawat dii khrap | Polite greeting. |
| sentence-01-thank-you-kha | ขอบคุณค่ะ | Thank you. | khop khun kha | Polite thanks. |

## Exercises
| ID | Prompt | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |

| exercise-01-thank-you-kha | Say thank you politely using ค่ะ. | ขอบคุณค่ะ |
