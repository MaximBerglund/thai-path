# Lesson 6 – Places, Locations & Prepositions

## Learning Objectives

After this lesson you'll be able to:

- Describe where people and things are.
- Ask where something is.
- Use common Thai prepositions.
- Talk about everyday places.

---

# Grammar

## Rule #18 – อยู่ (yùu)

อยู่ is one of the most common words in Thai.

It often means:

- to be located
- to stay
- to live

Examples:

ผมอยู่บ้าน

I'm at home.

โรงแรมอยู่กรุงเทพฯ

The hotel is in Bangkok.

---

## Rule #19 – Asking "Where?"

Combine อยู่ with ไหน.

Example:

โรงแรมอยู่ไหน

Where is the hotel?

# Grammar Notes

Remember this pattern:

**Object + อยู่ + Location**

Examples:

กาแฟอยู่บนโต๊ะ

The coffee is on the table.

กระเป๋าอยู่ในรถ

The bag is in the car.

โรงแรมอยู่ที่นี่

The hotel is here.

---

# Mini Dialogue

A:

ขอโทษครับ โรงแรมอยู่ไหน

(khɔ̌ɔ-thôot khráp, rooŋ-rɛɛm yùu nǎi?)

Excuse me, where is the hotel?

B:

โรงแรมอยู่ข้างตลาด

(rooŋ-rɛɛm yùu khâang dtà-làat.)

The hotel is beside the market.

A:

ขอบคุณครับ

(khɔ̀ɔp-khun khráp.)

Thank you.

B:

ไม่เป็นไรครับ

(mâi bpen rai khráp.)

You're welcome.

---

# Thai in the Wild 🇹🇭

## ไม่เป็นไร

*mâi bpen rai*

Depending on the situation it can mean:

- No problem.
- It's okay.
- Don't worry.
- Never mind.
- You're welcome.

You'll hear this expression every day in Thailand.

---

# Pronunciation Focus 🎧

## ใกล้

(glâi)

Many learners accidentally pronounce this as two syllables.

Native speakers say it as one smooth syllable.
